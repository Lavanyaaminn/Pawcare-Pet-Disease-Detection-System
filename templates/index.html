About PawCare

Caring for Pets, Powered by Technology

PawCare is a web-based Pet Disease Detection System developed to help pet owners manage their pets efficiently. Users can securely store pet profiles, predict possible diseases using symptom matching, and maintain a history of previous predictions. The platform is designed with a clean, user-friendly interface to make pet healthcare management simple, organized, and accessible.
